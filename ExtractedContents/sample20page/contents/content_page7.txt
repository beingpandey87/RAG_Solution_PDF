3.4 Definition-Style Lists
PDF (Portable Document Format): A file format developed by Adobe to present
documents consistently across all platforms and devices.
PDF/A (PDF for Archiving): An ISO-standardized version of PDF designed for long-term
preservation of electronic documents.
PDF/X (PDF for Exchange): A subset of PDF designed for reliable prepress data
exchange in the printing and publishing industry.
PDF/E (PDF for Engineering): A subset of PDF intended for engineering documents,
supporting interactive 3D content.
PDF/UA (PDF for Universal Accessibility): A standard ensuring PDF documents are
accessible to people using assistive technology.
